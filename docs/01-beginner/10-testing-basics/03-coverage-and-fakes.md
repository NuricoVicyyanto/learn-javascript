# 10.3 Coverage and Fakes

> **Level:** Beginner · **Module:** 10 Testing Basics · **Status:** ⬜ Not started
> **Estimated time:** 75–90 min

## Why it matters

Coverage shows what ran, not what is correct. Fakes let you test failure paths without a real network or database.
