## Why it matters

JavaScript converts types implicitly in surprising places. Knowing the coercion rules and preferring strict equality removes a whole class of bugs.

## Common mistakes

- **Mistake:** Treating `[]`, `{}`, or `"0"` as falsy. Explain the cause and the fix in your own words.
