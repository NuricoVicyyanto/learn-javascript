> **Level:** Beginner · **Module:** 03 Language Basics · **Status:** ⬜ Not started
> **Estimated time:** 75–90 min

## Why it matters

Loops and branches are simple, but the wrong loop construct creates subtle bugs. Early returns and `for...of` are usually clearer than nested conditionals.
